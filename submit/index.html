We invite contributions from researchers, designers, practitioners, policymakers and community organisations engaging with the challenges and possibilities of creating gender-inclusive cities.

Please select the theme that best aligns with your contribution. We welcome a broad range of submissions, including academic research, methodological studies, policy and professional practice, and community-based projects.

Publication opportunities

All accepted abstracts will be included in an ISBN-registered Book of Abstracts, published under the University of Strathclyde Publishing imprint. The book will be made available before the conference.

Following the conference, selected full-length papers will be invited for publication in the British Academy Proceedings, subject to the relevant editorial and peer-review processes. This publication will also be open access and released at the end of 2027.
