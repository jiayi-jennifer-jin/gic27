Publication opportunities

The organising team plans to publish an ISBN-registered Book of Abstracts before the conference. The University of Strathclyde Publishing imprint is proposed, subject to final confirmation of publishing arrangements. Accepted abstracts are intended for inclusion, subject to author agreement and editorial checks.

Following the conference, selected contributors may be invited to develop full papers for a proposed volume in the Proceedings of the British Academy series. This would require a separate publication proposal, approval and peer review. Publication dates and open-access arrangements are not yet confirmed.
